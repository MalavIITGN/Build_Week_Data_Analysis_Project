## Key Risks

1. **Data quality risk:** NOVA migration and missing patient identifiers may affect some records.
2. **Interpretation risk:** Bookings, collected samples, and result rows represent different stages of the operational process.
3. **Business diagnosis risk:** Clinic booking declines are visible, but capacity, pricing, availability, and lead times were not tested.
4. **Scope risk:** The analysis does not cover all possible revenue, customer, or operational drivers.

**Overall status:** The principal growth and demand patterns have been quantified. Data-source validation and additional operational analysis remain necessary before attributing causes or finalising interventions.
