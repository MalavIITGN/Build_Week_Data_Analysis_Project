## Final Reporting Principles

1. Present measured growth and shortfalls as verified numerical findings.
2. Label proposed explanations as hypotheses until validated.
3. Separate booking activity from collections and result completion.
4. Highlight Jaipur as a priority and Chennai as a mixed clinic-versus-home pattern.
5. Avoid causal language until additional operational and data-quality checks are complete.

**Overall decision:** The current analysis is sufficient to identify the growth gap and prioritise further investigation. It is not sufficient to establish the underlying causes of weak growth or prescribe a definitive operational remedy.
